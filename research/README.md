# Cross-project research

This directory is reserved for research whose conclusions inform more than one
repository.

Project-specific archaeology, source material, experiments, and findings
belong under that project's `research/` directory once the project has an
owner repository. The project README should explain why the research matters
and link to a research index that distinguishes:

- original sources and provenance;
- observed results;
- interpretation and open questions;
- executable or inspectable evidence;
- machine-local raw, licensed, mutable, or debug material.

The `davecarr1024` hub may retain a short pointer or cross-project synthesis,
but not a second working copy of the project's archive or research tree.

MoonRock now follows this rule. The train-book material owned by Paper
Railroad is the next project-specific research migration.
