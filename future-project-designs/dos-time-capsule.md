# DOS Time Capsule

## Seed

Turn the recovered personal software archive into a runnable, documented time capsule: a small set of DOS environments, source snapshots, binaries, screenshots, and first-person notes about what each project taught.

## Central experience

A future reader can choose a project, run it in an isolated emulator, see its output, inspect its source or binary boundary, and understand the next concept it made visible.

## Contents

- pinned emulator configurations
- source and binary provenance
- build/run transcripts
- screenshots and short recordings
- artifact-to-concept links
- personal recollections attached to the relevant experiments

## Guardrail

Preservation comes before modernization. Compatibility layers and clean-room ports may be added, but they must not overwrite or obscure the original artifact path.
